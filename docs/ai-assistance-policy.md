# AI assistance policy

This repository's own rules for AI-assisted open-source work.

- The upstream project's policy is binding and is read by the owner before any work starts. Record the clause
  and the date read in the candidate or contribution record.
- Where upstream prohibits AI-assisted code, AI-generated comments, or AI-assisted work on certain labels (for
  example good-first-issue items), no AI tool is used for that work, including drafting.
- Where upstream permits AI assistance, disclose it in the form upstream asks for (PR text, commit trailer).
- Issue and PR comments are written by the owner personally unless the project explicitly allows otherwise.
- The owner must understand and be able to explain every submitted line.
- AI output, including research output in this repository, is evidence to check, not authority.
