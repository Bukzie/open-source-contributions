## Reading the evidence references

The reports cite files such as `evidence/synthesis/boxed_mutmut_596.txt`. Those raw `gh` outputs (about 200
files, 7 MB) are **not published**. They contain third-party issue and PR comment text and unreviewed agent
output, and nothing in them has been sanitized. They are kept locally. Where a published claim matters, check it
again against the upstream link.
